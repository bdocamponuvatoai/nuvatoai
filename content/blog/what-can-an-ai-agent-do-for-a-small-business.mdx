An AI agent can take a routine job with clear steps, such as sorting new enquiries, drafting a first reply or moving information from a form into the right record, and work through it on its own, handing anything unusual to a person. It works best on jobs your team already does the same way every time.

This post is for owners of businesses of about ten to twenty people in the US, Canada and Argentina. Each section stands on its own, so read the one you need.

## What is an AI agent, in plain words?

An AI agent is software that is given a goal and a set of tools, and works through the steps to reach that goal.

A simple automation follows a fixed path: when this happens, do that. An agent can read what arrives, decide which of a few known paths it belongs to, and take the next step. It can read an email, work out whether it is a new enquiry, a complaint or an invoice, and act on each one differently.

The important part is the set of tools and rules you give it. An agent can only use the systems it is connected to, and only in the ways it has been allowed to.

## What kinds of jobs can an AI agent handle?

The jobs that suit an agent share a pattern. Something arrives, someone reads it, decides what kind of thing it is and does one of a few known things with it.

In a small business, that often looks like this:

- **Sorting incoming messages.** Reading new emails or form entries and labelling each one: enquiry, existing customer, supplier, spam.
- **Drafting first replies.** Writing a reply to a common question for a person to check and send.
- **Moving information between systems.** Taking the details from a form or an email and putting them into the right record, without someone retyping them.
- **Preparing a summary.** Pulling together what came in overnight, what is waiting and who it is waiting on.
- **Following up on things that stall.** Noticing that an enquiry has had no reply and reminding the person who owns it.

None of these is new work. They are jobs someone on your team already does, usually in between other jobs, and usually late in the day.

## How does an AI agent work, step by step?

Take one job, sorting new enquiries, and follow it through.

1. **A message arrives.** An email lands in the shared inbox, or a form is submitted on your website.
2. **The agent reads it.** It looks at what the person asked for and any details they gave.
3. **It decides what kind of message it is.** It matches the message against the categories you set up, such as new enquiry, existing customer or something else.
4. **It takes the agreed step.** A new enquiry is added to your records and the right person is told. An existing customer's message goes to whoever handles their account.
5. **It hands off what does not fit.** Anything it is unsure about goes to a person, with a note saying why.
6. **A person checks the work.** Someone reviews what the agent did, especially in the first weeks, and corrects the rules where it got something wrong.

Every step in that list is a decision your team already makes. The agent follows the same rules, written down.

## What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions in a conversation. A person types, it replies, and the job ends when the chat ends.

An AI agent works on a job. It can read what arrives from several places, take steps in the systems it is connected to and hand work to people. Some agents never talk to a customer at all; they sit behind the scenes and keep work moving.

## What should an AI agent never do alone?

Some decisions should always have a person in them, however routine they look.

- **Anything involving money.** Sending payments, issuing refunds or changing prices.
- **Promises to customers.** Agreeing dates, prices or exceptions that bind your business.
- **Anything hard to undo.** Deleting records, cancelling orders or closing accounts.
- **Sensitive conversations.** Complaints, legal questions and anything personal.

A well-built agent drafts, sorts and prepares. A person decides and signs off. That line should be written down before anything is switched on.

## Does an AI agent replace staff?

It changes what your staff spend their time on. The sorting, retyping and chasing that fills the gaps in a working day is the part an agent can take on.

The judgment stays with people: the customer who needs a phone call, the quote that needs a second look, the problem nobody has seen before. In a business of ten to twenty people, those are usually the jobs the team never has enough time for.

## What does a business need before it uses an AI agent?

Three things make the difference between an agent that helps and one that creates work.

- **A written process.** The agent follows rules, so the rules need to exist. If the job lives in one person's head, write it down first. Our guide on [what an SOP is and when a small business needs one](https://nuvato.ai/blog/what-is-an-sop) covers how.
- **Clear limits.** Decide what the agent may do on its own, what it may only draft and what it must hand to a person.
- **An owner.** Someone on your team checks its work, answers its hand-offs and updates the rules when the business changes.
